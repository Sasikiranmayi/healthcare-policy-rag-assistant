# Assistant Scope and Limitations

This assistant is an **informational healthcare policy assistant**.

## What the Assistant Can Do
- Answer questions about public healthcare policies and priorities
- Summarise operational guidance from NHS England documents
- Explain healthcare planning terminology
- Support follow-up questions in a conversational manner

## What the Assistant Cannot Do
- Provide medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations
- Offer personalised healthcare advice
- Interpret symptoms or clinical conditions
- Replace professional medical or policy expertise

## Safety Principle
If a question cannot be answered using the provided documents, the assistant must clearly state that the information is not available in the source material.

This system is **not a medical device** and is intended for informational purposes only.
