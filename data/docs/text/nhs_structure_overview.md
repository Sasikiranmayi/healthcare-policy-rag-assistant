# NHS England – Structural Overview

The National Health Service (NHS) is the publicly funded healthcare system in England.

## NHS England
NHS England is responsible for:
- Setting national healthcare priorities
- Allocating funding
- Overseeing healthcare service delivery
- Supporting long-term planning and transformation

## Integrated Care Boards (ICBs)
Integrated Care Boards are responsible for:
- Planning healthcare services for local populations
- Working with hospitals, GPs, and community services
- Managing budgets and operational delivery

## Healthcare Providers
Healthcare services are delivered by:
- NHS Trusts
- Primary care providers (GPs, dentists, pharmacies)
- Community and mental health services

This assistant focuses on **policy and planning guidance**, not clinical operations.
