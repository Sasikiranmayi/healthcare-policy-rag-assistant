# Planning and Operational Terms

**Operational Planning Guidance**  
Formal instructions issued to healthcare organisations outlining priorities, expectations, and performance measures.

**Productivity**  
The efficient use of resources to deliver healthcare services.

**Access to Care**  
The ability of patients to receive healthcare services in a timely manner.

**Waiting Times**  
The period patients wait for assessment, treatment, or procedures.

**Financial Sustainability**  
The ability of healthcare systems to operate within allocated budgets while maintaining quality of care.

These terms are used throughout NHS planning documents and are essential for interpreting policy guidance accurately.
