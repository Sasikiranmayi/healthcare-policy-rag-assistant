# Healthcare Glossary

**Elective Care**  
Planned medical treatment that is not urgent or emergency-based.

**Integrated Care Board (ICB)**  
An NHS organisation responsible for planning and funding healthcare services in a local area.

**Primary Care**  
The first point of contact for patients, including general practice, dentistry, and pharmacy services.

**Urgent Care**  
Medical services for conditions that require prompt attention but are not life-threatening.

**Operational Planning**  
The process of translating healthcare priorities into actionable plans for service delivery.

**Health Inequalities**  
Differences in health outcomes between populations due to social, economic, or geographic factors.
